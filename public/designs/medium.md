## Overview

Medium's design prioritizes reading experience above all else. Serif fonts for body text create a literary feel. The green accent is used sparingly for positive actions.

## Colors

Green (#1A8917) as the sole accent on clean white. The palette is extremely minimal to avoid distracting from content.

## Typography

Mixed typography: sans-serif for UI elements, serif (Georgia) for article body. This creates a clear distinction between interface and content.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** prioritize readability above all else
2. **Don't** add visual elements that distract from reading
3. **Do** use serif fonts for long-form content
4. **Don't** use more than one accent color
