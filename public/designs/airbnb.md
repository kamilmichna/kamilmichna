## Overview

Airbnb's design is warm, inviting, and trustworthy. The coral primary creates excitement while clean whites and generous spacing make browsing comfortable. Rounded corners add approachability.

## Colors

Airbnb coral (#FF385C) on clean white. The warm accent creates emotional connection appropriate for travel and hospitality.

## Typography

Custom Circular font with strong weights. Headlines are bold and confident, body text is comfortable for extended browsing.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** use coral to create emotional connection
2. **Don't** make the interface feel cold or corporate
3. **Do** embrace generous spacing and rounded corners
4. **Don't** clutter — let listings breathe
