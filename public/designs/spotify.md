## Overview

Spotify's design is bold, energetic, and instantly recognizable. The signature green on black creates a premium entertainment feel. Heavy typography and card-based layouts organize vast content libraries.

## Colors

Dark surfaces (#121212) with Spotify green (#191414) as the accent. The green appears sparingly but powerfully for primary actions and active states.

## Typography

Extra bold headlines (900 weight) create visual impact. The system uses weight contrast to establish hierarchy in dense content layouts.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** use green sparingly for emphasis
2. **Don't** add unnecessary visual noise
3. **Do** embrace dark backgrounds for immersion
4. **Don't** use thin weights — bold is the brand
