## Overview

Netflix's design is cinematic and immersive. Dark backgrounds recede while content comes forward. The signature red creates urgency and excitement without overwhelming the viewing experience.

## Colors

Near-black (#141414) with Netflix red (#E50914) as the sole accent. The red appears sparingly for CTAs and highlights.

## Typography

Clean, readable fonts that don't compete with visual content. Headlines are bold but not heavy.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** let content be the star — the UI should disappear
2. **Don't** use red everywhere — it loses impact
3. **Do** embrace dark backgrounds for immersion
4. **Don't** add visual noise that distracts from content
