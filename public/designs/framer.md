## Overview

Framer's design is bold, modern, and animation-forward. Massive typography creates dramatic hierarchy. The dark background with blue accent feels premium and creative.

## Colors

Pure black (#000000) with electric blue (#0055FF) as the primary accent. High contrast creates a premium, cinematic feel.

## Typography

Inter at extreme sizes with tight letter-spacing. The 72px headline with -0.04em tracking is signature Framer.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** embrace massive typography for impact
2. **Don't** be afraid of whitespace — it's dramatic
3. **Do** use animation purposefully
4. **Don't** add visual noise — bold simplicity wins
