## Overview

Plausible's design is clean, honest, and distraction-free. The purple-blue primary creates a trustworthy feel. The interface is intentionally simple, reflecting the product's privacy-focused philosophy.

## Colors

Purple-blue (#5850EC) as the primary accent. Muted, professional palette that doesn't demand attention.

## Typography

Inter at standard sizes with comfortable reading weights. The system prioritizes clarity over style.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** keep the interface simple and honest
2. **Don't** add dark patterns or manipulative UI
3. **Do** let data speak through clean visualization
4. **Don't** overcomplicate — simplicity is the brand
