## Overview

Twitter's design is dense and information-focused. The timeline format requires compact, scannable elements. Blue serves as the primary interactive color, appearing on links, buttons, and active states.

## Colors

Twitter blue (#1D9BF0) on white. The palette is extremely minimal — blue for actions, black for text, gray for secondary.

## Typography

System fonts at small sizes for maximum density. Tweets need to be scannable in rapid succession.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** keep tweet density high for scanning
2. **Don't** add visual noise — content is king
3. **Do** use blue consistently for interactions
4. **Don't** use large font sizes — the timeline is compact
