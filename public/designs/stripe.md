## Overview

Stripe's design system is characterized by clean lines, generous whitespace, and a sophisticated purple-blue primary color. The system balances professionalism with approachability, using subtle gradients and smooth transitions to create a premium fintech experience.

## Colors

The palette centers around Stripe's iconic purple (#635BFF) paired with deep navy (#0A2540). Teal accents (#00D4AA) provide positive reinforcement, while the light surface (#F6F9FC) creates breathing room.

## Typography

Inter is used throughout with careful weight distribution. Headlines use bold weights with tight letter-spacing for impact, while body text maintains excellent readability at 400 weight.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** use the primary purple for CTAs and key interactive elements
2. **Don't** overwhelm with too many colors — let purple lead
3. **Do** maintain generous spacing between sections
4. **Don't** use shadows excessively — rely on subtle borders
