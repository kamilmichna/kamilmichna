## Overview

Cal.com's design is clean, friendly, and professional. The purple accent creates a modern feel while maintaining approachability. The system balances density with readability.

## Colors

Purple (#7C3AED) on clean white. Professional but not cold. The palette supports a scheduling-focused UI.

## Typography

Inter with moderate weights. The system prioritizes readability for calendar and scheduling interfaces.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** use purple for primary actions
2. **Don't** make scheduling feel stressful — keep it calm
3. **Do** maintain consistent spacing
4. **Don't** add unnecessary decoration
