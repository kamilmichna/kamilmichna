## Overview

Vercel's design system is bold and confident. Pure black backgrounds with stark white text create maximum contrast and a premium feel. Heavy font weights and tight letter-spacing give headlines a commanding presence.

## Colors

Binary palette: pure black (#000000) and white (#FFFFFF). No gradients, no softness. The simplicity is the statement.

## Typography

Inter at 800 weight with aggressive negative letter-spacing creates Vercel's signature bold headlines. The contrast between heavy headlines and light body text is dramatic.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** embrace negative space — let content breathe
2. **Don't** add decorative elements — the typography is the decoration
3. **Do** use the heaviest weights for headlines
4. **Don't** use colors other than the core palette
