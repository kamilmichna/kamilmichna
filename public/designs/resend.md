## Overview

Resend's design is clean, confident, and developer-focused. Black and white with minimal accent colors creates a premium, no-nonsense feel. Heavy typography creates visual impact.

## Colors

Pure black and white. The restraint is the statement — no accent colors, just contrast.

## Typography

Inter at heavy weights with tight tracking. The typography carries the entire visual identity.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** embrace extreme minimalism
2. **Don't** add color for color's sake
3. **Do** use typography as the primary design element
4. **Don't** sacrifice functionality for aesthetics
