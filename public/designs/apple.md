## Overview

Apple's design language is defined by restraint and precision. Massive whitespace, system fonts, and careful hierarchy create an experience that feels effortless and premium.

## Colors

Extremely limited palette. Blue (#0071E3) is the only accent, used sparingly for interactive elements. Everything else is black, white, or gray.

## Typography

SF Pro at carefully calculated sizes with precise letter-spacing. The 56px headline with -0.03em tracking is Apple's signature.

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** use massive whitespace — it's not empty, it's breathing room
2. **Don't** add decorative elements — simplicity is luxury
3. **Do** use system fonts for authenticity
4. **Don't** use more than one accent color
